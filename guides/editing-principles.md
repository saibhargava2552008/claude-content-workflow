# Core Principles of Short-Form Video Editing

## 1. Pacing & Momentum
- The first 3 seconds define the hook: avoid logos, intros, or delayed reveals.
- Cut dead air and breath pauses ruthlessly between phrases.
- Introduce a visual change (cut, scale punch, graphic reveal, or angle switch) every 2.5 to 4 seconds.

## 2. Motivated Cuts
- Every transition should serve a communicative purpose:
  - Punch-in: Emotional intensity or critical emphasis.
  - B-roll: Illustrating an abstract mechanism or real-world example.
  - Graphic / Diagram: Clarifying data relationships or technical steps.

## 3. Caption Engagement
- Keep captions centered in the vertical safe zone (above the bottom 250px).
- Highlight the currently spoken word (karaoke style) to anchor viewer eye movement.
- Break sentences at natural grammatical phrases, not arbitrary character counts.
