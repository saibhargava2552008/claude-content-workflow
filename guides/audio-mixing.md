# Audio Mixing for Short-Form Video

Clear, intelligible audio is the foundation of audience retention. Viewers will tolerate mediocre video quality, but poor audio leads to immediate abandonment.

## Target Standards
- **Dialogue / Voice-over**: Integrated loudness around -14 LUFS (streaming standard) with peaks kept below -1.0 dBFS.
- **Background Music**: Set 14 to 20 dB below dialogue so spoken words remain crisp and effortless to comprehend.
- **Sound Effects (SFX)**: Mixed intentionally to accentuate cuts, reveals, and key statements without masking dialogue.

## EQ & Processing Recommendations
1. **High-Pass Filter**: Roll off sub-bass below 80 Hz on voice tracks to eliminate rumble and handling thuds.
2. **Clarity Boost**: Subtle presence lift between 2.5 kHz and 4.5 kHz enhances voice clarity on mobile phone speakers.
3. **De-Essing**: Tame harsh sibilance in the 6 kHz to 9 kHz region.
4. **Compression**: Use moderate compression (2:1 to 3:1 ratio) to maintain consistent dialogue volume across dynamic delivery.
