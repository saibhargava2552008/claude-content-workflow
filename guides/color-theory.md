# Color Theory & Contrast in Video Design

Visual hierarchy in vertical video requires high contrast and purposeful color harmony.

## Guidelines
1. **Legibility & Safe Areas**: Captions and key text overlays must have high contrast against dynamic backgrounds. Use dark drop shadows or semi-transparent backing pills.
2. **60-30-10 Rule**:
   - 60% Dominant background tone (e.g. deep slate or dark charcoal).
   - 30% Structural surface color (cards, containers, mockups).
   - 10% Vibrant accent color (key metric highlights, active words, CTA buttons).
3. **Color Consistency**: Limit palette to 2–3 core hues per video to establish a cohesive aesthetic.
4. **Mobile Screen Optimization**: Ensure elements remain clearly distinguishable under ambient light on OLED and LCD screens.
